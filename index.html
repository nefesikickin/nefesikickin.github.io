Klip Botu

A private, single-operator tool that turns highlights from Kick live streams into short vertical videos and publishes them to our own social media accounts.

What it does

  1. Watches the public Kick live stream and chat of the streamers we work with.
  2. Detects candidate moments (chat activity spikes), transcribes the audio and scores each moment with an AI editor.
  3. Cuts the best moments into vertical clips with subtitles, a title and source credit (streamer name and Kick link).
  4. Uploads approved clips to our own YouTube channel (via the YouTube Data API, videos.insert only), Instagram account and TikTok account.

Who uses it

Only the operator of this project, on accounts the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, it has no sign-up, and it never accesses other people's YouTube, Instagram or TikTok accounts.
