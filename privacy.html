Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Klip Botu ("the app") is a private tool run by a single operator. It publishes short highlight clips to the operator's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account. Contact: nefesikickin@gmail.com.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: an OAuth 2.0 token with the youtube.upload scope for the operator's own channel. It is used only to upload videos (videos.insert). The app does not read, collect or store any other YouTube data, and it does not access any other user's account.
  • Instagram (Meta) and TikTok: access tokens for the operator's own accounts, used only to publish videos and their captions.
  • Kick: public live-stream video and public chat messages of the streamers we clip. Chat messages are processed only to detect highlight moments; usernames are not published.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to create and publish highlight clips. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties other than the platforms the clips are published to (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) and the AI services that score clips (which receive short transcripts and still frames, not account data).

5. Storage and deletion

  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in local files that are never published.
  • Raw stream recordings are kept in a rolling buffer and deleted automatically (minutes to days). Chat samples are deleted together with the stream data.
  • Stored API data (tokens and upload IDs) is deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days, or immediately on request.
  • You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at Google security settings. Revocation stops all access immediately.

6. Cookies

The app has no user-facing website features, sets no cookies on users' devices, and uses no tracking.

7. Streamers and viewers

If you appear in a clip and want it removed, email nefesikickin@gmail.com and we will delete it from our accounts.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
